## Notes on the shape

- Four questions are shown here to keep the example readable. A full scan
  tests eight, and lite mode tests four.
- Every buying question names the assistant-level outcome and only the
  competitors that actually appeared in an answer.
- Any factor scored from indirect evidence says so in its Evidence sentence.
- A factor with no evidence scores low and states the observation, such as
  the zero for Reddit and Quora presence.
- Each `Fix prompt` stands alone: it names the domain and needs no extra
  context from the chat.
- The report uses no em dash, no JSON, and no HTML.
